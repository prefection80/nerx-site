    Generator Synchronization Challenge by Doug Millner

    Synchronize as many generators to the grid as possible. You have 20 seconds for each generator, plus any time remaining from the previous successful sync! Get bonus points for accuracy (closer to 0° angle and 0°/s slip).

    Use 'A' to accelerate Clockwise.
    Use 'L' to brake.
    Press SPACE when the needle is within the green target lines (±5° of 12 o'clock) AND the slip speed is low (±5 deg/s).

    Hold 'A' or 'L' for greater acceleration! Each successful sync makes the game more challenging in multiple ways:

    • HALVED INERTIA: Generator inertia is divided by 2 each time!
    • DOUBLE CONTROL POWER: Controls are TWICE as responsive each time!
    • HIGHER SLIP SPEEDS: Initial speed varies more widely
    • NO SPEED LIMIT: The needle can reach EXTREME rotation speeds!

    Good luck - you'll DEFINITELY need it!
