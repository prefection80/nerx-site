Waitlist

People who wanted a seat on this date when it was full. When a seat frees — someone reschedules away, you release or void a ticket, or you add capacity — the first person in line is emailed and the seat is held for them for 24 hours (it shows as held on the calendar and cannot be taken by anyone else). If they do not book in time it passes to the next person.

Class materials

The links a student gets with their ticket for this training: the presentation shown in class, reference material to read alongside it, a recording afterwards. Anyone whose ticket is booked on this date, or was used to attend it, sees them under My Ticket whenever they look their code up — and on the certificate page, the join page and the class-time reminder email. Paste a share link (Drive, Dropbox, the site); the title is what the student clicks and the note is one optional line under it.

Courses

Every session date sharing a name is one course. Set the certificate subtitle, PDH hours, price, length, seats and meeting link here once and apply them to every date — or only the upcoming ones. Renaming renames the dates too. Nobody is emailed (the time and status are not touched). Class materials have their own editor above.

Import emails (paste a list)

Paste emails in any format — commas, semicolons, one per line, or Outlook "Name <email>". Duplicates and anyone already on the list are skipped; anyone who previously unsubscribed is left opted-out.

Open ticket price

The price of a seat bought without a date ("Buy Seats — Schedule Later"). A ticket bought at this price can book any session costing that much or less.

Signups (and moves onto a date) close this many hours before a session starts. Sessions inside the window show as locked on the calendar and cannot be booked or paid for. 0 keeps them open until class begins; you can always seat someone yourself from the roster.

hours before class

Shortly before each class, every booked student is emailed a reminder with a Join class link (plus the class materials). The link checks them in for their PDH credit and opens the meeting, same as the button on the ticket page. The roster shows when each seat was reminded. Sessions without a meeting link skip the reminder and email you instead. 0 turns reminders off.

minutes before class

After each class, everyone who attended is emailed once: their certificate link and the class materials. 0 turns it off.

minutes after class ends

A seat offered to someone on the waitlist is held for them this long before it passes to the next person (never past the signup cutoff).

Class record sheet

Printed on every class record (and available to a board on request): who taught it, and the provider / sponsor numbers for the states you are registered with.

Weekly backup email

Once a week the whole ticket database — every ticket with its code and payment, every session, the certificate register, the waitlist, the mailing list and the full activity log — is emailed as a ZIP so a copy lives in your inbox if this server is ever lost. Keep the latest one; it supersedes the rest.
