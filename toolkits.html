Sequence Components Toolkit

A hands-on visualizer for symmetrical (sequence) components — the analytical backbone of unbalanced fault analysis. Drag the phase or sequence sliders and watch the phasors respond in real time, then see any unbalanced three-phase set resolve into its positive-, negative-, and zero-sequence networks. Step through worked fault conditions with the underlying math shown right alongside the diagram — the picture and the equations, finally in one place.

  • Real-time phase & sequence sliders
  • Positive / negative / zero decomposition
  • ABC & CBA rotation
  • Single line-to-ground & phase-to-phase faults
  • Polar ↔ rectangular · degree ↔ radian tools
  • Runs in your browser — nothing to install
