Step Potential Equation (IEEE 80):

Estep = (1000 + 6Csρs) (0.116 / √ts) (W / 50)

  • ρs = Crushed Rock Resistivity
  • Cs = Surface Layer Factor
  • ts = Fault Clearing Time (s)
  • W = Body Weight (kg) (standard formula references 50 kg)

Leg currents are computed from the simplified circuit bridging the two foot voltages via foot + body resistances.
