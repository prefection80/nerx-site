nerx-signals

Live component demo — phasor animation, oscillography, and the calc engine that feeds them. One 20 KB script, no dependencies.

1 · Phasor — rotating, with waveform projection

Three-phase set. Drag |Iₐ| and ∠Iₐ; the arrows and the sine traces follow. The spinning arrow is the waveform.

2 · Scope — a relay event record

Phase currents on a cycle grid; 3I₀ is a derived residual — dead flat until the ground fault at the trigger, then the whole story. The X/R slider changes the DC-offset decay.

3 · Calc → Phasor — a fault, solved and drawn

An SLG fault's phase currents, computed by calc.faults() and drawn as phasors. Change Z₀ and watch the ground path open up.

4 · Impedance — R–X plane (distance relaying)

Mho zones 1–3 and a live apparent-impedance point orbiting through the characteristics, like a power swing.

5 · Coordination — time-current curves

A 51 relay against a fuse on log-log axes. Drag pickup / TD and the fault line; watch which device is faster.

6 · Stability — power-angle & equal area

Pre-fault, fault, and post-fault P–δ curves with the accelerating (red) and decelerating (green) areas, and the rotor angle swinging.

7 · Spectrum — harmonics & THD

Add 3rd / 5th / 7th harmonics and watch the waveform distort and the THD climb. Bars are % of fundamental.

8 · One-line — animated power flow

A radial feed with current flowing along the conductors; thickness tracks magnitude, dots track direction.
