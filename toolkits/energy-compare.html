Elaborated Mike Tyson Punch Energy Calculation

We model the punch as a mass of fist and arm acceleration. Using:

    m = 100 kg    // approximate mass contributing to punch
    v = 4.5 m/s   // estimated punch speed

Compute kinetic energy:

    E = ½ m v²
      = ½ × 100 kg × (4.5 m/s)²
      = 50 × 20.25
      = 1,012.5 J

Therefore, each punch delivers around ≈1,010 J (≈1.01 kJ) of energy.
