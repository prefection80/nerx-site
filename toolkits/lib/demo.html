nerx-signals

Live component demo — phasor animation, oscillography, and the calc engine that feeds them. One script, no dependencies.

1 · Phasor — rotating, with waveform projection

Three-phase set. Drag |Iₐ| and ∠Iₐ; the arrows and the sine traces follow. The spinning arrow is the waveform.

2 · Scope — a relay event record

Phase currents on a cycle grid; 3I₀ is a derived residual — dead flat until the ground fault at the trigger, then the whole story. The X/R slider changes the DC-offset decay.

3 · Calc → Phasor — a fault, solved and drawn

An SLG fault's phase currents, computed by calc.faults() and drawn as phasors. Change Z₀ and watch the ground path open up.

4 · Impedance — R–X plane (distance relaying)

Mho zones 1–3 and a live apparent-impedance point orbiting through the characteristics, like a power swing.

5 · Coordination — time-current curves

A 51 relay against a fuse on log-log axes. Drag pickup / TD and the fault line; watch which device is faster.

6 · Stability — power-angle & equal area

Pre-fault, fault, and post-fault P–δ curves with the accelerating (red) and decelerating (green) areas, and the rotor angle swinging.

7 · Spectrum — harmonics & THD

Add 3rd / 5th / 7th harmonics and watch the waveform distort and the THD climb. Bars are % of fundamental.

8 · One-line — animated power flow

A radial feed with current flowing along the conductors; thickness tracks magnitude, dots track direction.

9 · Power — power factor, and what it actually costs

V and I with the angle between them, p(t) = v·i underneath, and the P–Q–S triangle. Drag the pf: the middle panel ripples at twice line frequency, averages to P, and the red lobes are energy handed back to the source. At pf = 1 they disappear.

10 · Decompose — the transform as a construction

Not a matrix: rotate by the a-operator, add tip to tail, divide by three. Pick the row and drag the step, or let it run. Unbalance the set with |Vₐ| and watch V₂ grow out of nothing.

11 · Estimator — why the relay needs a cycle

A DFT window slides along the record; the phasor it produces is on the dial, and its magnitude against window position is on the right. The estimate is only true once the window is entirely inside the fault. Add dc offset with X/R and watch a one-cycle window overshoot.

12 · Compare — two figures, one canvas

Wipe, split or crossfade between two components. This is the -orig/-rev slide pair as a single slide with a handle you drag in front of the room. Same geometry on both sides, so the difference is the only thing that moves.

13 · Callout — annotation that survives the PDF

An arrow and a label anchored to a point in the figure, not to a pixel — and revealed on a step. Ink is great live and gone in the export; this isn't. Drag step to build them up the way a fragment would.
