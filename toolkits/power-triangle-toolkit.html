4-Quadrant Power Triangle & Animated Waveforms

P = Real Power  |  Q = Reactive Power  |  S = Apparent Power

Key Equations:

$$S = \sqrt{P^2 + Q^2}$$

$$\theta = \arctan\left(\frac{Q}{P}\right) \quad,\quad \mathrm{PF} = \cos(\theta)$$

$$P = S\cos(\theta) \quad,\quad Q = S\sin(\theta)$$
